GOOD QUESTIONS.

A few things
before you clear.

A little clarity for your clean slate.

What exactly does LaterZero do?+

It automates removing videos from your YouTube Watch later playlist, one at a time. Start it from your Chrome toolbar, then follow the live counter and timer in the corner of the page.

Can I stop it halfway through?+

Yes. Press Stop in the progress panel to stop removing more videos. Videos already removed stay removed. Try the button in the demo above to see how it works.

Does it delete videos from YouTube?+

It removes videos from your Watch later playlist. The original videos remain on YouTube, and you can add them to Watch later again yourself.

Can I undo a cleanup?+

There is no automatic undo. Make sure you’re ready to clear your list before you start. You can stop the process at any time, but that won’t restore videos already removed.

Does it work on mobile?+

LaterZero is being built for Google Chrome on desktop. It doesn’t run inside the YouTube mobile app.
